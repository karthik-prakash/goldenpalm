Where Tradition Meets Artistry

Namaste! I'm Shruthi, the heart and hands behind Golden Palm. My journey with Mehendi began as a childhood fascination — watching intricate patterns bloom on my grandmother's hands during festivals. What started as innocent doodles on paper evolved into a deep, soulful passion that has been my life's purpose for over 8 years.

Every design I create tells a story. From the delicate paisleys of a bridal Mehendi that symbolize fertility and love, to the playful patterns for baby showers that celebrate new beginnings — each stroke carries meaning, tradition, and my personal artistic touch.

I specialize in blending traditional Indian motifs with contemporary aesthetics, creating designs that are both timeless and trendy. Whether it's an elaborate bridal masterpiece or a minimal chic design, I pour my heart into every creation.
